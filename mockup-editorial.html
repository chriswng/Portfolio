Point of view

Most corporate climate programs fail at the same joint: the gap between the number on the slide and the data underneath it.

Mandatory disclosure — AASB S2, ISSB, CSRD — is quietly turning sustainability reporting into a financial-controls problem. The organisations that treat it that way early will spend the next decade making strategy; the ones that treat it as a communications exercise will spend it restating numbers. The credibility of a net zero commitment is set by its inventory, not its ambition.

For consumer-facing companies, the hardest version of this problem lives in the supply chain: Scope 3 numbers built on spend-based proxies that no buyer would bet a margin on. The work that matters is unglamorous — supplier data pipelines, boundary decisions, documented methodology — and it is exactly the work most programs defer.

Case study — sample of three

A net zero pathway model the board could interrogate.

Downer EDI · Group climate strategy · FY26
The situation
The Group’s climate strategy needed a pathway the board could engage with directly, and business units needed a common reference for tracking abatement progress.
The call
Make every assumption traceable or leave it out. Each lever sourced to published data — DCCEEW grid projections, NVES fleet trajectories, CSIRO pathways — so a director’s “what if the grid is slower?” has an answer that moves, and an analyst can trace any number to its source.
The outcome
Presented to the board by senior leadership and adopted as the internal reference for business-unit abatement tracking — operated without me in the loop.

  • One accent colour replaces the five-colour SaaS palette. Restraint is the credibility signal for fashion and corporate-strategy audiences.
  • No decorative animation. No grain, contours, scramble effects, counters, or role cycling. Whitespace and type scale carry the hierarchy.
  • Charts are figures, not widgets. Each case study gets one static, annotated graphic that states its conclusion. The interactive model survives as a single appendix artifact.
  • The warming stripes stay — reduced to a quiet horizon line above the footer. It is the one decorative element that is about the subject matter.
  • Reading order: positioning → point of view → three case studies → career facts → contact. Built for the 60-second recruiter first.

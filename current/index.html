The recalculation

Fifteen FY27 abatement initiatives, 1,930 tCO₂-e claimed, headed for board sign-off.

As the independent Group Reviewer I recalculated every initiative from first principles. I found an unvalidated 288.5 t line, a 250-vehicle count discrepancy, 675 t of missing haircuts, and unsupported rollover lines, and I documented each with cell-level evidence.

1,930 t recalculated to a credible 1,111–1,930 t range, with 43 annotated review comments on the source file.

first-principles workpaper · executive brief · 43 cell-level comments

I built a strategic model integrating 130 decarbonisation initiatives and four published frameworks: DCCEEW Table 42, CSIRO Net Zero Pathways, the NVES Act 2024, and market-based Scope 2, with revenue-intensity forecasting layered across the horizon.

An FY20–FY50 pathway across 130 initiatives, built for board presentation by senior leadership.
